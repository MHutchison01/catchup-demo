Product pipeline

From a working app to the app they can’t stop using.

The core loop ships and works — calendars in, overlaps out, one nudge to a plan. This is the build order that turns that into a habit: every feature below is tied to a real friction, ranked by the value it unlocks against the effort to build it.

21 features 3 build waves 1 brand-craft track 10 feel & polish upgrades Grounded in the shipped v1
The why

The frictions we’re killing

Catch Up only earns a place on the home screen if it removes a specific pain. Every feature in the pipeline points back to one of these.

“When’s everyone free??”
40 messages and a dead poll to pick one night. Coordination is the whole job.
“We should catch up soon.”
Good intentions that never become a date. Plans fizzle in the chat.
“Oh, you were free too?”
Both open on Thursday, neither knew. Overlaps stay invisible.
“I’ll just leave them on read…”
Saying no is awkward, so nobody answers at all.
“It’s been months.”
Friendships drift because the friction to meet is higher than the friction to skip.
“Where do we even go?”
Decision paralysis on the venue kills momentum right at the finish line.

Wave 1 · Now

Finish the promise

What the first real users hit within a week — the gaps between “it works” and “it’s complete.” Highest value, mostly small-to-medium lifts.

The core surface

A week that comes alive

The week grid is the heart of it — so make it feel alive. Swipe across weeks, drag to paint your free time, and watch a cell turn gold the moment everyone lines up.

HighM
Plans fizzle

Plans that flex

Move a time or venue and everyone gets a quiet “still good?” re-confirm — instead of the plan quietly dying when one thing clashes.

HighM
Missed overlaps

Set-and-forget free time

“I’m always free Thursday nights.” Recurring availability means more real overlaps surface with zero weekly upkeep.

HighM
Get friends on

Invites that just work

A share link (WhatsApp, Messages) that drops a friend straight into your circle — install, sign up, and the overlap is already there. The growth engine.

HighM
Out of sight

Home & lock-screen widgets

Your next plan and this week’s gold slots, glanceable without opening the app. The single strongest retention lever for a time app.

HighM
Privacy · App Store

Your data, your call

In-app account deletion and a plain “here’s exactly what each circle can see” view. Deepens the trust promise — and Apple requires deletion anyway.

HighS
Assumed out

Free right now

Opt in to show a circle you’re about — a quiet, circle-only signal that fades on its own. So a friend who’s free but under the match line still gets seen and pinged, instead of being assumed out. Never public, never a status to keep updating.

HighM
Wave 2 · Next

Deepen the habit

Once the loop is complete, these make Catch Up something people open on their own — proactive, personal, spontaneous, and rewarding to come back to.

Drifting

The weekend nudge

A Sunday digest: “2 gold slots with the Uni crew this week.” The app reaches out before the drift sets in.

HighS
Signal, not noise

Notifications, done right

Every reply, friend join, and plan change lands as a clear, actionable alert — grouped in a tidy centre, tunable per circle and per type, answerable without opening the app. Never a buzz that isn’t worth it.

HighM
Where to go

Local spots, near everyone

Suggestions anchored to where you actually are — the good local pubs, cafes and restaurants a short walk from the crew’s midpoint, open now, weather-aware. The map and venue engine already run on your location.

HighM
Identity

Your profile, built out

More than a coloured dot: a photo, a line about you, your go-to spots and catch-up history — the identity that makes a circle feel like people, not initials.

MediumM
Commit · Revenue

Book it in-app

Reserve the table without leaving Catch Up. Removes the last drop-off — and it’s the doorway to venue revenue.

MediumL
Full picture

Every calendar

Outlook / Microsoft (creds already in hand) plus multiple calendars per person — so “free” means genuinely free.

MediumM
Available ≠ up for it

Quiet one, or big night?

Tag a free slot with the kind of catch-up you’ve got in you — quiet ↔ up for anything — as you paint it, not as a status to maintain. So “free” carries intent, and nobody has to guess whether it’s pizza-on-the-couch or a big-night invite.

HighM
Anticipation

Plan-day Live Activity

On the day, a lock-screen countdown with who’s in — the little hit of anticipation that makes the plan feel real.

MediumS

Your year in catch-ups

A gentle end-of-year look back — who you saw, the streaks, the gold nights. Shareable, and quietly motivating.

MediumS
Reach

Android & iPad

The crew is never all on iPhone. Expo makes Android close to free; iPad widens the surface. Reach unlocks the network effect.

MediumL
Look & feel

Brand & craft evolution

The identity is already strong — teal, coral, violet, gold on paper, with Fraunces and a privacy-first voice. This track deepens its expression rather than replacing it: more range, more signature moments, more warmth. Runs alongside the waves, a little in each release.

Colour

Palette, evolved

Keep the accessible core and the eight person-hues; add depth — subtle gradients for the gold “it’s happening” moment, richer dark-mode tones, tints that carry meaning. Always AA-contrast, always colour-plus-shape.

Motion

Moments worth animating

The gold match, the plan confirming, the mark drawing itself — give the few emotional beats a signature motion and a little sound, so the app has moments people remember.

MediumM
Warmth

Illustration & empty states

Bespoke art where the app is quiet — the empty circle, the “you’re set” screen, the year in review. Warmth beyond geometry, in the brand’s own hand.

MediumM
Identity

Icon & themes

Alternate app icons and seasonal themes (a tidy Plus perk), plus a refined icon set across the tabs — small touches that make it feel crafted, and personal.

MediumS
Cross-cutting

Feel & polish

The details that separate “a working app” from one that feels considered. Small on their own; together they’re the difference people can feel but can’t name. Woven through every wave.
